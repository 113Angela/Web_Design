No Poverty

GOAL 01 / 17 Sustainable Development Goals

A quiet change is noticed during lunch time.

You notice that one of your classmates has recently stopped bringing lunch to school.

You start to realize this may not be a temporary situation.

Over time, you realize that this is not a temporary situation — it may be due to financial difficulties at home.

What would be the most appropriate and respectful action?

A
Tell the whole class about the situation and suggest raising money.
B
Decide not to interfere because it is their personal matter.
C
Quietly give them food every day without telling anyone.
D
Talk to them privately and help them find formal support.
